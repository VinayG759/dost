# Dost: decisions log

Every decision Vinay has made about Dost, in one place. **Every deliverable (submission, prompt, pitch, code) is checked against this list, line by line, before it is called done.** Add a line here the moment a new decision is made.

## Product
1. **Platform: an Android mobile app**, installed on the user's phone. Not a website or PWA.
2. **Multilingual.** Users speak their own language, mixed the way they really talk (Kannada-English, Hinglish). Kannada is Gangamma's language; for the hackathon, Hinglish + Kannada done well.
3. **Users: older people and blind or low-vision people.** Every feature works with eyes closed.
4. **Natural friend voice and personality**: warm, informal, filler words, like a real friend. This is the key part.
5. **Not always listening in the background.** The user switches Dost on; while on, it wakes when spoken to by the name the user chose for it.
6. **Dost speaks first**: medicine reminders, "yaad rakhna" reminders, the quiet-day check-in.
7. **Only what is in our control.** No claims about things Android or the hardware won't allow.

## Health
8. **The doctor briefs Dost directly**; Dost reads it back for the doctor to confirm.
9. **The son confirms every doctor visit**: Dost sends him photos of the prescription and medicines, plus a summary. Medicines on the written prescription start reminders straight away, marked unconfirmed. Risky changes (stopping a medicine, a doubled dose, anything not on the prescription) wait for his yes.
10. **Never doses or diagnoses.** Dost repeats only the doctor's instructions. General friend-style food tips ("sihi kammi maadi") are fine, but are never presented as the doctor's words unless the doctor said them.
11. **Medicines are named by how they look** (size, colour, shape) and by her own names ("sugar maatre"), never chemical names.
12. **"Idu sarina?"**: she shows a tablet before taking it, and Dost confirms or corrects. If tablets look alike, it asks for the strip.
13. **Reports are read through the camera and compared with earlier ones.**
