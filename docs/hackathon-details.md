## Round 1: Warm-up (online)

"One idea. A voice-first India." Three activities, released one a week, worth **100 points** in total. You must finish each activity to unlock the next one. Each activity goes through a quality check that either passes or comes back as "needs changes", with feedback. You can edit and resubmit.

| Activity | Opens | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea | 5 Nov | Submit a voice-first product idea for India |
| 2. Prompt | 13 Nov | Write the prompt you'd use to build a prototype of the idea |
| 3. Impact | 20 Nov | Describe the idea's impact |

Word limits for Activities 2 and 3 are only "proposed" so far.

### Activity 1: Idea (from the submission screen)

- **Length:** 50 to 500 words. **Due:** 26 Nov.
- **Submission:** one response per team. Anyone on the team can edit and save the draft, but **only the lead can submit**. If a teammate saves while you have the draft open, you are asked to reload so nobody's work is overwritten.
- **Prompt:** "Describe a voice-first product idea for people in India. Say who has the problem, where and how often it hurts, and why speaking is the main way people would use your product."

**What the idea should cover:**
1. **The problem:** one specific problem people in India face, e.g. paying an electricity bill or booking a clinic visit.
2. **Who has it, where and how often:** the people who face it, the setting, and how often it hurts.
3. **The voice-first product:** what people say to it and what it does, step by step.
4. **Why voice comes first:** why speaking is the main way people use it, not a feature added on top.

**How the quality check works:**
- An AI check reads the idea when you submit. If it passes, Activity 1 is complete and Activity 2 unlocks on 13 Nov.
- It checks that this is a real, specific idea, not empty, a joke, gibberish or a copy of the question.
- **It looks most at whether voice is essential to using the product, then at how specific the problem is.**
- The same check scores the idea out of **25 points** towards the Round 1 total.
- The page suggests submitting the idea by speaking it through Wispr Flow.

### Notes for writing the Idea

- **The trap is part 4.** "An app, plus you can also talk to it" loses on the criterion that matters most. Ideas score well when typing isn't a real option for the user, because:
  - they can't read or write well (or not in English or the app's script),
  - their hands are busy (shopkeeper, driver, farmer, nurse),
  - they can't see the screen (visually impaired or older users), or
  - the task is naturally spoken (calls, dictated reports).
- **Test:** if the voice part were removed, would the product still make sense? If yes, voice is a feature, not the core.
- **Be concrete.** Name a city, a type of person, and a number for how often it happens. The check rewards specific detail over broad claims like "millions of Indians".
- **Submit before 13 Nov** so Activity 2 unlocks on schedule.
- **Our rules:** the idea starts from a real person's pain or a domain we have touched. Check competitors, and answer who pays, how much, and why now, before writing.

### Activity 2: Prompt (from the submission screen, 13 Nov)

- **Length:** 80 to 800 words. **Due:** 27 Nov (the screen says 27 Nov, although Round 1 is listed as closing 26 Nov; aim for well before either).
- **Task:** "Write the prompt you would give an AI app builder to create a working prototype of your idea: the users, the voice flow step by step, what it says back, and how it handles noise, accents and mixed languages."
- One response per team; only the lead submits. The Activity 1 idea is shown on the same screen for reference.
- **What the prompt should cover:**
  1. **Your users:** who they are, and the languages and accents they speak in.
  2. **The voice flow, step by step:** what people say at each step, and what the product says or shows back.
  3. **When things go wrong:** noise, accents, mixed languages and misheard input, and what the product does in each case.
  4. **Enough detail to build:** specific enough that two builders would produce nearly the same prototype.
- **How it's evaluated:** an AI check confirms it's a real prompt someone could give an AI app builder. **Scoring weighs the voice flow most, then handling things going wrong, then how buildable it is. Up to 50 points** toward the Round 1 total. Passing unlocks Activity 3 on 20 Nov.
- **Draft:** `prompt-submission.txt` (723 words).
- An AI quality check reads it on submit and gives feedback; edit and resubmit if it needs changes.
