Track: Everyday access. Built by voice with Wispr Flow.

Build Dost, a voice-first PWA — a Kannada-speaking friend for older people in India who cannot read English. She talks, Dost talks back. Nothing on screen needs reading. Camera steps use spoken guidance; blind users describe pills by touch instead.

USERS
Gangamma, 70, Bengaluru, diabetic. Speaks Kannada mixed with English ("sugar", "BP", "tablet"), older accent ("shugar", "tablate"), sometimes Hindi. Slow speech, mid-sentence pauses, loudspeaker. Doctor: English or Kanglish, interacts with Dost only during visits, no app. Son Ravi: caregiver/payer, separate dashboard on his phone. Dost speaks Kannada to her, English to the doctor. STT/TTS packs are swappable for other Indian languages.

DATA MODEL
Per patient: medicines (name, mg, purpose_kn, visual descriptor, reference photos, meal anchor, dose window minutes, status: approved|awaiting-son), visits (date, instructions[], next check-up), lab values (date, test, value, unit, ref range, previous), contacts (spoken name, relation, phone), enrolled voice profile, learned meal times, interaction log (timestamp, speaker-verified, type).
Seed: Metformin 500 mg "sugar maatre" "chikka bili gundu" after dinner, 15-min window; Amlodipine 5 mg "BP maatre" "chikka pink" after breakfast; fasting sugar 150 mg/dL; son "maga" Ravi; check-up 15 Feb; dinner 8:30 pm.

SCREENS (two separate devices)
1. Gangamma: huge talk button (tap anywhere). Camera opens by voice command. Dost's face shows listening/thinking/speaking.
2. Ravi: medicines awaiting check (photo, doctor's words, Approve/Something's wrong), doses taken/missed, sugar trend. Push notification on every alert and new report.
3. Demo controls (hidden): jump to 8:45 pm, next day 10 pm, reset. Calls and alerts simulated on Ravi's screen, noted in UI.

SETUP (first use, by the son)
Adds medicines with photos of tablet and strip, enters meal times, records her voice for speaker verification. Dost proposes a descriptor per medicine; she confirms or corrects.

VOICE FLOWS

1. Doctor visit. She says "Doctor jothe maathadu." Dost: "Doctor hattira iddaara?" No or no doctor within 10 seconds: "Doctor hattira iddaaga heli." Doctor speaks; Dost extracts medicines, doses, timing. Reads back in English: "Metformin 500 mg, once after dinner, three months. Correct?" Doctor corrects any parts; Dost updates all and reads the full list again until confirmed. "Show the prescription." Camera reads it; mismatches read aloud, doctor chooses. Doctor gone: mismatches flagged to son. Saved "awaiting son." Ravi's screen: Approve marks approved, Dost tells her; Something's wrong pauses reminders, Dost offers to call him. Unverified reminders say "maga nodthiddaare." Dost never starts, stops or changes a dose on its own.

2. Medicine time. Anchored to meals: dinner 8:30 triggers "after dinner" at 8:45. Dost speaks first: "Oota aytha?" "Haudu": "Eega aa chikka bili gundu maatre tagolli." "Illa": snooze 20 minutes. She shows a tablet: "Idu sarina?" Dost compares only against her enrolled medicines and the dose due within its window. Right: "Haudu, adhe." Wrong: "Illa, adu BP maatre." Not sure: "Strip torisi." Not in her medicines: "Ee maatre nanage gottilla, Ravi ge heLona?" "Tagonde" logs the dose. No confirmation by 15 min: one reminder. By 30 min: push alert to son. She can initiate anytime — "Idu sarina?" matches whatever is next due.

3. Report. "Report nodu." On-device edge detection plays pre-cached cues (no server trip): "swalpa edakke", "hattira", "haan, saaku." Stable one second: auto-capture. "Sugar 180 ide, kaleda sala 150 ittu. Doctor helidru sihi kammi maadi antha." Son gets a push notification with value and trend.

4. Calling. "Maganige phone maadu." Dost: "Ravi ge call maadla?" "Haudu": call rings on his phone.

5. Quiet day. Only her enrolled voice counts. No verified voice by 10 pm: "Ivattu maatte aadilla, ella sari ideya?" Two tries, 10 min apart. Then push and call to son: "Amma ivattu maathadilla."

6. Questions and chat. "Doctor enu helidru?" or "Maatre yaavaga?" — only from saved visits and schedule. Not saved: "Nanage gottilla, Ravi ge kelona?" General chat ("Ivattu thanda ide"): warm reply, steers back: "Haa, thanda ide. Maatre tagondra ivattu?"

WHEN THINGS GO WRONG
Pauses under 2 seconds: not her turn ending; never cut her off. Not understood: "Gottaglilla, innondu sala heli?" Still unclear: two choices. Low-confidence number or medicine: read back before saving. Mixed Kannada/English/Hindi: fuzzy-match accented words to medicine and medical test names from her list. Noise under 300 ms: ignore. "Haa", "hmm", "houdu" while Dost talks: not interruptions. Real interruption: stop audio instantly, remember only what she heard, answer her, repeat any unfinished medicine instruction fully. Echo cancellation on; Dost ignores its own voice. Blurry photo: "Hattira hidi"; never guess; look-alikes need the strip. Slow network: pre-recorded "hmm, ondu nimisha" within 300 ms. Reply target under one second. Never diagnose or suggest doses.

BUILD
React PWA developed with Wispr Flow. Sarvam Saarika STT and Bulbul TTS, streaming, speed configurable per user. Claude with tool-calling (get_schedule, log_dose, check_pill, save_prescription, read_report, call_contact, alert_son). On-device OpenCV.js for camera guidance with pre-cached cue clips. FastAPI, Supabase Mumbai.
