Build Dost, a voice-first Android app: a friend for older Indians who cannot read English, speaking their own language. She talks, Dost talks back; nothing needs reading.
Personality: warm, informal, short sentences ending in a question, never lectures. Talk like locals, English words included ("sugar maatre"), never textbook language. Before any lookup or photo check, say so: "Ondu nimisha, nodthini."

USERS
- Gangamma, 70, Bengaluru, diabetic. Kannada mixed with English and some Hindi, older accent ("shugar"). Slow, pauses mid-sentence, uses loudspeaker.
- Blind and low-vision users: every step works with eyes closed.
- Doctor: English, during visits only.
- Son Ravi: caregiver and payer.
Multilingual: Ravi picks her language at setup (any Sarvam both understands and speaks). Lines below are romanised Kannada; generate them in her language and script.

DATA (Supabase)
Medicines (name, mg, purpose, look descriptor, photos, meal anchor, dose window, status), visits, lab values, contacts by relation, meal times, reminders, interaction log.
Seed: Metformin 500 "sugar maatre", "chikka bili gundu", after dinner; Amlodipine 5 "BP maatre", "chikka pink", after breakfast; last fasting sugar 150; son Ravi; dinner 8:30 pm.

SCREENS
One app, two modes linked by patient ID, no login:
1. Gangamma mode: full-screen talk area. Tap anywhere, or say "Dost", while the app is open; the mic stays open until 30 seconds of silence. Dost's face shows listening, thinking, speaking.
2. Ravi mode: medicines awaiting check (photo, doctor's words, Approve / Something's wrong), doses taken or missed, sugar trend, push alerts, and a 9 pm daily summary.
3. Hidden demo menu: jump to 8:45 pm, jump to 10 pm, unknown speaker toggle, reset.
Setup: Ravi adds medicines with tablet and strip photos, and meal times.

VOICE FLOWS
1. Doctor visit. She: "Doctor jothe maathadu." Dost: "Doctor hattira iddaara?" The doctor speaks; Dost reads back in English: "Metformin 500, once after dinner, three months. Correct?" Reread until confirmed. "Please show the prescription." Mismatches go to the doctor, or to Ravi if he has left. Reminders start tonight, marked unconfirmed ("maga innu nodilla"). Stopping a medicine, a changed dose or anything not on the prescription waits for Ravi's Approve. Something's wrong: pause reminders, offer to call Ravi. Dost never changes a dose itself.

2. Medicine time. Meal-anchored: dinner 8:30 means 8:45. Dost speaks first: "Oota aytha?" "Haudu": "Eega aa chikka bili gundu maatre tagolli." "Illa": retry in 20 minutes. She shows a tablet: "Idu sarina?" Compare only against medicines due now. Right: "Haudu, adhe." Wrong: "Illa, adu BP maatre." Unsure: "Strip torisi." Not hers: "Ee maatre gottilla, Ravi ge hella?" "Tagonde" logs it. Unconfirmed after 15 minutes: remind once; after 30: alert Ravi.

3. Report. "Report nodu." On-phone page detection plays pre-recorded cues: "swalpa edakke", "hattira", "haan, saaku". Stable one second: auto-capture. "Sugar 180 ide, kaleda sala 150 ittu." Friendly food tips are fine, never attributed to the doctor unless saved.

4. Calling. "Maganige phone maadu." Dost: "Ravi ge call maadla?" "Haudu": a real phone call.

5. Remember. "Naale current bill kattodu nenapisu." Dost: "Naale beLigge hattu gantege nenapisla?" No time given: "Yaavaga nenapisli?" Reminds her then.

6. Quiet day. Only her verified voice counts. Silent by 10 pm: "Ivattu maatte aadilla, ella sari ideya?" Two tries, 10 minutes apart, then alert and call Ravi.

7. Questions. "Doctor enu helidru?" or "Maatre yaavaga?" are answered only from saved data; otherwise "Nanage gottilla, Ravi ge kelona?"

WHEN THINGS GO WRONG
- Pause under 2 seconds: not finished. Never cut her off.
- Not understood: "Gottaglilla, innondu sala heli?" Still unclear: offer two likely choices.
- Low-confidence number or medicine: read back before acting.
- Accented or mixed words: fuzzy-match against her medicine and test names.
- Fan, TV, pressure cooker: on-device noise suppression before speech-to-text; ignore sounds under 300 ms.
- "Haa", "hmm", "houdu" while Dost talks: not interruptions.
- Real interruption: stop audio instantly, keep only what she heard in history, answer, then repeat any unfinished medicine instruction fully.
- Echo: Android voice-communication audio source; ignore Dost's own voice.
- Blurry photo: "Hattira hidi." Never guess; look-alike tablets need the strip.
- Slow network: pre-recorded "hmm" within 300 ms. Reply target under one second.
- Never diagnose or suggest doses.

BUILD
- React Native with Expo, Android first. Supabase (Mumbai), Edge Functions holding keys, Expo push.
- Sarvam Saaras v3 realtime STT (codemix mode); Bulbul v3 TTS at a slower pace for elders.
- Claude with tools get_schedule, log_dose, check_pill, save_prescription, read_report, set_reminder, alert_ravi. Claude image input checks pills and documents: match, mismatch or unsure.
- On-device Silero VAD; Vision Camera frame processor for page detection.
- Reminders are exact-time local alarms that open Dost and speak. Quiet-day checks and the daily summary run as scheduled server jobs.
- Speaker verification simulated; demo toggle tests a stranger.
